dist/models holds encoders converted from pretrained models, and classifier heads trained for this project.

Encoders and ImageNet heads
  mobilenetv4_conv_small  from timm/mobilenetv4_conv_small.e2400_r224_in1k (Hugging Face)
  mobilenetv3_small_050   from timm/mobilenetv3_small_050.lamb_in1k (Hugging Face)
  Both model cards state the Apache License 2.0; a copy is in LICENSE-APACHE-2.0.txt.
  Weights and architecture come from the timm project (Ross Wightman) and the MobileNet papers.
  Changes made here: BatchNorm folded into the convolutions, a 1x1 layer added in front to convert the input normalisation,
  weights stored as 16-bit floats, classifier moved to a separate head file with its weights multiplied by the mean feature norm.

Training data of those weights
  They were trained on ImageNet-1k, whose terms limit the data itself to non-commercial research. The license of the weights does not
  settle this question. Get legal advice before commercial use.

scenes14.orh
  Trained here on the training images listed in demo/tm/manifest.json and demo/tm/manifest2.json (public domain, CC0 and CC BY).
  The manifests hold the title, author, license and source page of every image.

textmap.orh
  Linear map to the space of OpenCLIP ViT-B-16 laion2b_s34b_b88k (MIT), fitted here on the same training images.

orasis2
  Encoder and ImageNet head start from timm/mobilenetv4_conv_small.e2400_r224_in1k (Apache License 2.0). They were fine-tuned here
  (colab/orasis2.py) on the COCO 2017 train and unlabeled images to follow OpenCLIP ViT-B-16 laion2b_s34b_b88k (MIT), and its textmap.orh
  was trained with them on those images. COCO images keep the licenses their authors gave them on Flickr. The ImageNet question above
  applies here too, because training started from those weights.
